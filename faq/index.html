Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the Islnd browser extension.

What is Islnd?

Islnd is a browser extension that puts a small floating bar at the bottom of every web page. From it you can type an address or a search, reopen pages from your history, go back, forward, reload and run quick commands that start with /.

Which browsers does Islnd support?

Islnd is available for Mozilla Firefox and Microsoft Edge. A Google Chrome version is coming soon.

How do I open Islnd?

Click the domain shown in the bar or press ⌘L (Ctrl+L on Windows and Linux). Press ⇧⌘L to minimize or restore the bar.

Does Islnd send my history anywhere?

No. History suggestions are read only inside the browser, and favicons come from the browser's own cache, so Islnd makes no requests to the sites you visit.

Can I customize the bar?

Yes. In the settings you can choose position (top or bottom), size, width, light or dark theme, which buttons to show and your home page.

Can I use keyboard shortcuts for commands?

Yes. In the settings › Commands you can assign a shortcut to each command. By default Minimize Islnd is ⇧⌘L, Copy URL ⌥⇧⌘C and Screenshot ⇧⌘S.
