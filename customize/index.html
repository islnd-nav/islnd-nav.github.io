Make it yours.

Every part of the bar can be adjusted from the settings page. Try it: the Islnd bar on this page changes as you pick.

Position and size. Put the bar at the bottom or top of the page, pick default, large or extra large, and set its width.

Theme. Automatic follows your system; or force light or dark.

Buttons. Pick up to 8 buttons and put them in the order you like, left or right of the address: back, forward, reload, home, new tab, close tab, bookmark page, screenshot, commands and more. Add your own links too: each one becomes a button with the site's favicon. The home button opens the new tab page or a page you choose.

Commands. Choose which / commands appear and give each one its own shortcut.

Full list in the settings documentation.
