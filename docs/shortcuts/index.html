Shortcuts

  • ⌘L opens Islnd (Ctrl+L on Windows and Linux); the commands and links have their own shortcuts, such as ⇧⌘L for Minimize Islnd (see Commands). Islnd handles them itself, on web pages (embedded editors included), before the page's and the browser's own shortcuts. Browsers never let an extension take over their own keys on their own pages, so there they keep the browser's meaning. On excluded sites, where Islnd is not loaded, and during full-screen video, all of them are left to the page.
  • To change them, open the settings › Shortcuts (⌘L) or Commands (the others), click the key and press the new combination: it needs ⌘ (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) together with another key, and it can add ⇧, ⌥ or ⌃. Esc cancels, ⌫ removes a command's shortcut. Not accepted: the keys for editing text (⌘A, ⌘C, ⌘V, ⌘X, ⌘Z), a combination already used by Islnd or by one of the browser's shortcuts listed in Commands, and the browser's keys that never reach a page, such as ⌘T or ⌘W.
