Features

Everything in one small bar.

Islnd replaces trips to the browser's toolbar with a bar that floats at the bottom of every page.

Address & search

Click the domain or press ⌘L (Ctrl+L on Windows and Linux). Type a URL or a search: it uses your browser's default search engine.

History suggestions

Up to 10 matching pages from your history, with favicon, title and full address. ↓ ↑ to pick, ↩ to open, ⌘↩ in a new tab.

/ commands

Type / to run quick commands such as copy URL, screenshot, full page screenshot, close other tabs and minimize Islnd.

Buttons you choose

Back, forward, reload, home and new tab to start with; add close tab, bookmark, screenshot, commands and more, or your own links, up to 8, in the order you like.

Stays out of the way

Islnd slides away when you scroll down and comes back when you scroll up or move the mouse to the bottom of the window.

Private by design

History is read only inside the browser. Favicons come from the browser's cache, so Islnd makes no requests to the sites you visit.
